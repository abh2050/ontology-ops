## Owner decision taken during M1
On the full data, the literal late-open rule kept orders that never get a delivery timestamp late open forever. At the literal pick (2018-08-25), only about 5 % of the late open set was ever delivered.

The owner chose to apply the default purchase window to the scan and to add a 30-day overdue cap to `late_open_order`. This was done before operational-commerce-v1 is frozen and is recorded as D-003. The cap boundary is tested by `test_overdue_cap_boundary`.

## Findings that affect later milestones
- **Multi-seller late open orders are very rare in Olist**: 0 at the demo as_of, and 0–3 on any day. Multi-seller deduplication and the split-grant scenarios are therefore exercised mostly by fixtures-v1. The demo narrative must not imply otherwise.
- Carrier assignment is uneven (B and D hold 74 %). That follows from the specified hash over (seller_state, customer_state), where SP dominates, and it is expected.
