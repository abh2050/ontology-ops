# Pilot decision · October 18, 2026

Recommend the deterministic workspace (B0). The assisted candidate fails the predefined task-success and recall gates and does not improve over B0 by the required meaningful margin. B0 achieved 1.000 task success on gold-v1; the best candidate cell achieved 0.581. The accepted-causal-claim, authorization, and approval checks pass, but those controls are necessary conditions rather than evidence of incremental assistance value.

The benchmark derives expected answers from deterministic queries of the same kind B0 uses. Its perfect baseline makes it unsuitable for proving AI benefit. Narrative clarity and human speed could matter, but no timing participants have been measured. A model-generated narrative must not be treated as an established cause of delay.

The next study should test B0 with real operators, using the existing timing protocol, within explicit seller grants. Measure time, errors, grouped-case eligibility, operational volume, and recurring effort before estimating financial return. Any new assistance benchmark needs owner review and a separate freeze; leave gold-v1 unchanged. No new paid evaluation is recommended under the current benchmark.

The measured grouping potential (603 orders in 139 groups at the default Olist snapshot) can also be obtained deterministically. It is neither observed case reduction nor an AI-specific benefit. The conditional economic projection in `business_case.md` remains assumption-driven and does not change this recommendation.

The build supports only internal investigations and recipient-free internal drafts. A reviewer must independently hold every target's seller scope and cannot approve their own proposal. The candidate is retained as an evaluated prototype, not a recommended autonomous operational actor.

Evidence: [results](results.md), [business case](business_case.md), [M5 gate](progress/M5.md), and [timing protocol](timing_protocol.md).
